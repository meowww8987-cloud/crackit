8 Design Decisions
1
Subject as a "Hero Card" — not a list row
Each subject becomes a prominent card with a colored halo icon (36×36), gradient tint background, and a glowing border. Active subject gets a radial glow in the top-right corner so you instantly see what you're studying.
2
Mini progress ring (30px) replaces the thin bar
A circular ring with the subject's color shows % complete at a glance, with a soft glow. The number sits inside the ring. Far more legible than a 1.5px bar — and visually anchors the right side of the header.
3
Time + Done count shown inline
"1h 20m / 2h 30m" and "3/5 done" appear under the subject name in tabular numerals. You see actual vs expected in one glance — no need to expand anything to know where you stand.
4
"Studying now" pulsing chip
When a session is active for this subject, a small pulsing chip with a live dot appears in the header. No more guessing which subject the floating widget belongs to — the card itself tells you.
5
Chapter pills strip — no more nested accordions
Chapters become horizontally scrollable pills: filled pill = in-progress, green check pill = done, dashed "+ Chapter" = add new. Tap a pill to expand that chapter inline (only one open at a time). Saves 60% vertical space vs the old chevron-accordion.
6
Inline expanded chapter — targets stay in context
When you tap a chapter pill, the TargetCards appear inline in a recessed panel (darker background, soft border) — not in a separate section. The expanded header shows "2/3 done · 40m left" so you always know what's left.
7
"Start next" primary action button
A gradient Start button in the card footer launches a focus session for the next incomplete target in this subject — skipping the expand → find → tap dance. The button label even tells you which target it'll start ("Start next: Notes — Friction").
8
Add (+) tertiary button + long-press menu
A small icon button next to Start adds a target to this subject (opens AddTargetSheet pre-filled). Long-press the card opens a context menu: collapse all chapters, mark all done, jump to syllabus, archive.
✕ Problems with current SubjectSection
Flat 1.5px progress bar — invisible at a glance
Subject + Chapter look identical (both just chevron + text)
Two-level nested accordions = too many taps
No way to start a session from subject level
Active subject only differs by a faint glow-pulse class
No "time vs expected" shown — only "X/Y done"
Chapter header + bar takes a full row each (wasted space)
No quick add-to-subject — must open full sheet
